# Component model register

The component deck is assembled from this directory. The reference deck continues to use the original model definitions. Values below are typical or fitted unless qualified. The component benches use 25 °C. Camera runs default to 27 °C.

| Manufacturer and orderable part | Source and model class | Validity and limitations |
|---|---|---|
| Hamamatsu S5971 silicon PIN photodiode | Local SPICE junction model in `devices.lib` plus an external optical current sink. Hamamatsu gives 0.44 A/W at 660 nm, 70 pA typical and 1 nA maximum dark current at 10 V, and 3 pF typical at 10 V and 1 MHz. [Datasheet](https://www.hamamatsu.com/content/dam/hamamatsu-photonics/sites/documents/99_SALES_LIBRARY/ssd/s5971_etc_kpin1025e.pdf) | Low-speed charge collection under reverse bias. An abrupt depletion junction with assumed 0.7 V built-in potential gives Cj0 = 3 pF × √(1+10/0.7) = 11.73 pF. The default saturation current matches the dark-current datum. The forward emission coefficient 1.2 and 5 Ω series resistance are assumptions. The fit at 10 V does not independently establish accuracy at 3 V, forward overload, or other temperatures. Transit time, lateral carrier transport and photon statistics are omitted. |
| Advanced Linear Devices ALD1106PBL quad NMOS array | Four-terminal level-1 MOS channels used as pass devices. The published ALD1106 model gives geometry, threshold, transconductance, body effect, modulation and junction capacitances. [Measured-model presentation](https://tianshiwang.github.io/talks/2015-05-11--PHLOGON-talk-for-NEEDS-v2.pdf) · [ALD datasheet](https://www.aldinc.com/pdf/ALD1116.pdf) | Geometry 20/10 μm, threshold 0.7 V. Adjusted KP from 225 to 245 μA/V² to bring small-signal on-resistance within the specified 500 Ω maximum. The simulated 479 Ω is above the 350 Ω typical value. Each overlap is 0.1 pF, entered per metre of channel width. Assumed 30 nm oxide and 0.6 pF gate-package capacitance match approximately 1 pF total low-bias input capacitance. Bulk junction saturation current is set to the 10 pA typical off-current datum. A 10 TΩ gate shunt represents terminal leakage. This is a part-specific nominal fit with quasi-static MOS charge storage. Charge partition, switch injection and subthreshold leakage lack measured waveform validation. Pin V− connects to ground, V+ to the 9 V domain. Channel terminals and gate levels must stay within the device ratings. |
| Texas Instruments TL081CP JFET-input op amp | Original TI TL081 model `TL081.301` from archive SLOJ069, used for all buffers, differential and gain amplifiers. TI publishes a named macromodel with a JFET input pair, compensated gain stages, poles, supply-dependent output clamps, output resistance and current limiting. This is the IC macromodel exception. No public transistor-level design was identified from TI. [TI model archive](https://www.ti.com/lit/zip/sloj069) · [Datasheet, legacy TL08xC tables](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tl081.pdf) | The model header specifies ±15 V. The camera uses ±7.5 V for ground-centred headroom and to limit overdrive of the 9 V mux. Operation there is an extrapolation. At ±15 V TI lists 65 pA typical bias, 3 mV typical offset, 1 TΩ common-mode resistance, at least ±12 V swing into 10 kΩ, and 8 V/μs minimum slew. The CP grade is in the 5.25 MHz typical GBW group in rev. O. The bench gives 11.8 V/μs at 2 kΩ and 100 pF. The camera-supply bench gives 22.5 pA bias and −0.053 mV follower error at 2 V. The symmetric model understates offset spread. The internal supply resistor also limits the accuracy of quiescent-power estimates. Noise density and device mismatch have not been validated. |
| NXP Semiconductors 1N4148, SOD27 | Vendor PSPICE subcircuit from the PySpice model collection, retained as `1N4148.lib`. Uses a junction diode with series resistance, capacitance, transit time, reverse breakdown and a vendor-specified reverse shunt. [Model copy and original provenance](https://github.com/PySpice-org/PySpice/blob/master/examples/libraries/diode/switching/1N4148.lib) · [Nexperia-hosted datasheet](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/1N4148_1N4448.pdf) | Compression uses the forward characteristic at sub-mA current. At 10 mA the standalone bench measures 0.732 V, inside the 1 V maximum. Recovery and noise are outside the validation. SPICE pin 1 is the anode, while physical package pin 1 is the cathode. |

The S5971 and ALD cards are local implementations. ALD describes a separate design-kit library. A publicly retrievable manufacturer card for the packaged ALD1106 was not identified during component selection, so this model uses the published device fit. [ALD model library description](https://www.aldinc.com/pdf/ALDmacro.pdf) The TL081 and 1N4148 files preserve the supplied vendor text. Neither vendor file contains an explicit redistribution grant. Vendor terms apply to those files and to generated decks embedding them. Local availability should not be taken as clearance for publication. The research model and datasheets remain their authors’ work.

## Compatibility and verification

TI's original file has SHA-256 `74746c9e184824a7665d9b24c767f5f8cfabc77fbb4893115966dee531b08cf5`. The loader removes its trailing DOS EOF marker. It expands the linear EGND polynomial into two series voltage-controlled voltage sources and FB into five parallel current-controlled current sources with the original signs and gains. This avoids depending on XSPICE polynomial code-model initialization. A standalone ngspice comparison of the original TI polynomial model and the port produced a maximum absolute difference of 4.2 × 10⁻¹⁵ in complex follower voltage gain across 193 AC points. No extra control source supplies an acquisition transfer function. The 1N4148 file is unmodified, SHA-256 `db2ee7a82fd6398c2abb233b03e27f76fa2df1aca33a252adc24696b8764f8a7`.

Run `python -m experiments.components` to regenerate standalone decks and numerical results. The 1 MHz junction and leakage checks recover the S5971 fitting data. They check implementation and units. Independent physical validation would require measurements at additional bias points. [Component bench outputs](../../../results/phase2/components/components.csv)
